I'm a Computer Engineering undergraduate at AURAK, born in Gujarat, India and now based in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. I'm deeply fascinated by the question of how machines learn — not just that they do, but why and how well.

My academic and personal interests converge on machine learning foundations, representation learning, and optimization-based methods. I want to understand how learning algorithms generalize, how efficient AI systems are designed, and how data can be transformed into meaningful insight. I finished my first semester with a GPA of 3.78/4.0 and earned a place on the Dean's List.

Through hands-on engineering projects — from competitive system design to logic-driven C++ applications — I've built strong instincts for abstraction, constrained optimization, and structured problem solving. I'm actively seeking research-oriented opportunities in AI/ML and plan to pursue graduate studies in the field.
