I'm a Computer Engineering undergraduate at AURAK, born in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia and now based in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. I'm deeply fascinated by how machines learn — not just that they do, but why and how well.

My academic and personal interests converge on machine learning foundations, representation learning, and optimization-based methods. I want to understand how learning algorithms generalize, how efficient AI systems are designed, and how data can be transformed into meaningful insight. I earned a place on the Dean's List in my first semester and maintain a strong academic record.

I'm actively seeking research-oriented opportunities in AI/ML and plan to pursue graduate studies in the field.
